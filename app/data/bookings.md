# Bookings & Cancellations

## How do I book a trip?
Search for a destination on the TravelZone homepage, pick your dates, and
select a package. You'll review the price breakdown, add any optional
extras (travel insurance, airport transfer), and confirm payment. You'll
receive a confirmation email with your booking reference within a few
minutes.

## Can I cancel a booking?
Yes. Go to "My Bookings" and select "Cancel Booking" next to the trip you
want to cancel. Cancellations made more than 14 days before departure are
fully refundable. Cancellations made between 7 and 14 days before
departure receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made less than 7 days
before departure are non-refundable, except for bookings with
Flexible Cancellation add-on, which are refundable up to 48 hours before
departure.

## Can I change my travel dates?
Date changes are allowed up to 10 days before departure, subject to
availability at the new dates' pricing. Go to "My Bookings", select
"Modify Dates", and choose your new travel window. A price difference (if
any) will be charged or refunded automatically.

## How do I add a traveler to an existing booking?
Open the booking under "My Bookings", select "Manage Travelers", and add
the new traveler's details. Additional travelers are billed at the
per-person rate for the selected package.
