# Payments & Refunds

## What payment methods are accepted?
TravelZone accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI, and PayPal.
Some regions also support Apple Pay and Google Pay at checkout.

## How long do refunds take?
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. The refund is
issued to the original payment method. Bank processing times may add a
few extra days before the funds appear in your account.

## Why was my payment declined?
Payments are commonly declined due to insufficient funds, an expired
card, or your bank's fraud-detection rules blocking an unfamiliar
merchant charge. Try a different payment method, or contact your bank to
authorize the transaction, then retry checkout.

## Can I get an invoice for my booking?
Yes. Every confirmed booking automatically generates a PDF invoice,
available under "My Bookings" > "Download Invoice". Invoices are also
emailed to the address on file at the time of booking.
