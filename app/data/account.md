# Account & App

## How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and select "Forgot Password". Enter the email
associated with your account and you'll receive a reset link valid for
30 minutes.

## How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes your
profile, saved favorites, and booking history after a 14-day grace
period, during which you can cancel the deletion by logging back in.

## Why am I not receiving confirmation emails?
Check your spam/junk folder first. If nothing arrives within 15 minutes,
verify the email on file under Settings > Profile is correct, and add
no-reply@travelzone.example to your contacts to prevent future emails
from being filtered.

## Is there a mobile app?
TravelZone is currently a responsive web app that works well on mobile
browsers. A dedicated native app is on the roadmap but not yet available.
